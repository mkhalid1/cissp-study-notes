# Frequently asked questions

These are the questions people search for most about the CISSP, mostly drawn from Google's "People also ask" results for US searches in November 2026, plus a few we hear often. Answers stick to what ISC2 states, and say so when an answer is our guidance.

## How long is the CISSP exam?

A maximum of 3 hours, with 100 to 150 items. Because the exam is adaptive, it can end as early as item 100 if the system is 95% confident of the result. There is no minimum time. See the [timing guide](timing.md).

## What is the passing score for the CISSP exam?

ISC2 sets the passing grade at 700 out of 1000 points. It is a scaled standard, not a percentage of items correct: the adaptive engine judges your ability from the difficulty of the items you answer correctly. See [how scoring works](scoring.md).

## Is CISSP changing in 2027?

Yes. ISC2 has published a new exam outline effective April 15, 2027. Domain 1 becomes Security Governance and Risk Management, and the weights become more even, for example Domain 1 moves from 16% to 13.6%. The exam stays at 3 hours and 100 to 150 items. If you sit before that date, study the current April 2024 outline. Details are in the [exam overview](test-overview.md#the-april-2027-outline).

## What are the requirements for taking the CISSP exam?

You can sit the exam without meeting the experience requirement. To become certified, ISC2 requires five years of cumulative, full-time experience in two or more of the eight domains, with up to one year satisfied by a relevant degree or an approved credential. If you pass without the experience, you become an Associate of ISC2 and have six years to earn it. See the [practitioners page](for-practitioners.md#experience-the-associate-path-and-endorsement).

## How do I prepare for the CISSP exam?

Start with the [ISC2 exam outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp/cissp-certification-exam-outline), find your weak domains with a diagnostic, study each domain's concepts, and practise many scenario items without going back. The habit that matters most is choosing the best answer for the organisation, not the fastest technical fix. These notes give you a [diagnostic](diagnostic.md), eight domain guides and [study plans](study-plans.md).

## How should I study for the CISSP?

Work domain by domain, weakest first. For each one: learn the key concepts, do practice items, and keep a miss log that records why the right answer was better than the one you chose. Add timed mixed sets regularly. This is our study guidance; ISC2 does not prescribe a method.

## Is CISSP very difficult?

It is demanding because it is broad and because most items test judgement rather than recall. The adaptive format also makes it feel hard: ISC2 says everyone gets about half of the items they see right, because items are pitched at your level. ISC2 does not publish a pass rate.

## Does CISSP expire after 3 years?

The certification runs in three-year cycles. To keep it, ISC2 requires 120 CPE credits per cycle and payment of an annual maintenance fee. ISC2 suggests an annual minimum to spread the credits out, but there is no annual requirement for certified members. If you meet those requirements, it renews; it does not expire simply because three years have passed.

## How much does the CISSP exam cost?

Prices vary by region and change over time, so check the current fee on [ISC2's own site](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp) before you book. Remember to budget for an annual maintenance fee once you are certified.

## How long does it take to get CISSP certification?

There are two clocks. Study time varies widely by background, and ISC2 does not publish a figure. After you pass, you have nine months to complete the certification application and endorsement. If you do not yet have five years of experience, you hold the Associate of ISC2 status and have up to six years to earn it.

## Is CISSP harder than Security+?

They are aimed at different stages of a career. The CISSP requires five years of experience to certify and tests management-level judgement across eight domains, so most people take it later in their careers. Compare the two using each certification body's own exam outline.

## Which certificate is the best for cybersecurity?

There is no single best one; it depends on the role you want. The CISSP suits experienced practitioners moving into security leadership, architecture or broad advisory roles; ISC2 lists roles from security analyst to CISO. If you are early in your career, check whether you can meet the experience requirement before choosing it.

## What are the benefits of CISSP certification?

ISC2 describes it as a credential for experienced security practitioners, managers and executives. It meets the ANAB ISO/IEC 17024 standard and is approved under the US Department of Defense directive DoDM 8140.03, which matters for some government and defence roles. Beyond that, the value depends on your market and the roles you target.

## Can I review or change my answers?

No. ISC2's adaptive exam does not allow item review. Once you finalise an answer, it cannot be reviewed or changed.

## Where can I practise for free?
