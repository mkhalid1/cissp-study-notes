## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not ISC2 statements:

- the per-item timing targets and checkpoints
- the manager's order of thinking
- the methods, traps lists, key concepts summaries and study plans
- all practice items and explanations, which are original

General security concepts such as the OSI model, RAID levels, backup types, CMM levels and common software weaknesses are standard industry knowledge, described here in our own words.
