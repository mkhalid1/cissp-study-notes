# Study plans

Be realistic first. The CISSP covers eight broad domains, and most candidates study for weeks or months before the exam. ISC2 does not publish a recommended study time, and the right amount depends on how much of the material you already use at work. The two plans below are not a substitute for that groundwork. They are for the **final week** and the **final 48 hours**, when the goal is to close gaps, practise the format and arrive rested.

Both plans assume you start with the [5-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weakest domains.

## Before the final week

If you have more time, a simple structure works: one domain guide per week, in the order of your diagnostic results from weakest to strongest, with a mixed timed practice set at the end of each week. Read the guide, do its practice items, keep a miss log, and read at least one deeper source for each domain, starting with the [ISC2 exam outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp/cissp-certification-exam-outline) itself.

## 7-day plan

Expect about 2 to 3 hours a day.

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Reread the [exam overview](test-overview.md) and the [practitioners page](for-practitioners.md). Retake the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Pick your three weakest domains. |
| 2 | Domain 1 plus weakest domain | Domain 1 is the largest at 16% and shapes how you answer everywhere else. Redo its 2 items, then your weakest domain's items. Log every miss. |
| 3 | Architecture and networks | Domains 3 and 4. Rebuild the crypto key grid, the model rules and the OSI table from memory, then redo both item sets. |
| 4 | Identity, testing and software | Domains 5, 6 and 8. Focus on the protocol table, the testing techniques table and the weakness-to-fix table. |
| 5 | First timed run | A full timed practice session with no going back, such as the [free test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cissp?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=cissp). Read the [timing guide](timing.md) first. Review every miss. |
| 6 | Operations and asset security | Domains 7 and 2. Write out the backup, site, RAID and DR test tables, and the sanitisation table. Redo items for your weakest domain from day 5. |
| 7 | Light review and rest | Read the traps lists for all eight domains, nothing else. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions in your miss log:

1. **What did the question really ask?** FIRST, BEST, MOST, or a fact? Which role and which stage?
2. **Why is the right answer better, not just correct?** Most misses on the CISSP pick a true option that is not the best one.
3. **What will I check next time?** One habit, such as "look for people safety first" or "write the keys out before choosing".

By day 6 the same two or three patterns will repeat. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the exam is the day after tomorrow.

### Day 1: close the biggest gaps

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 60 min | Read the "Key concepts" and "Traps" sections for your two weakest domains. |
| 3 | 45 min | Do those two domains' practice items and read every explanation. |
| 4 | 60 min | A timed mixed practice set without going back, such as the [free PrepClubs test](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cissp?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=cissp). |
| 5 | 30 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: the day before the exam

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 min | Read the [practitioners page](for-practitioners.md) section on thinking like a manager. |
| 2 | 45 min | Skim all eight traps lists. |
| 3 | 15 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 4 | Rest | No new material after mid-afternoon. Sleep matters more than one more chapter. |

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" exam questions.** The ISC2 item bank is confidential, recalled questions are unreliable, and sharing exam content breaks the ISC2 candidate rules and Code of Ethics.
- **Do not cram all night before.** Three hours of judgement items without going back punishes a tired brain.
- **Do not study only the domains you enjoy.** Every domain carries at least 10% of the average weight.
- **Do not practise with review switched on.** Train the way the exam works: one look, one decision.
