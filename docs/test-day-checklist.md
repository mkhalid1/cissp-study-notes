# Test-day checklist

A short list for the day before, the morning of the exam, and afterwards. Follow the instructions in your Pearson VUE confirmation and on ISC2's site where they differ from anything here: they are the rules that apply to you.

## The day before

- Confirm the test centre address, the start time and how long it takes to get there. ISC2 exams are delivered at Pearson Professional Centers and ISC2-authorised PVTC Select centres.
- Check that the name on your identification matches your registration exactly.
- Read the traps lists in the domain guides. Nothing new.
- Read the [timing guide](timing.md) once and fix the checkpoint numbers in your head.
- Stop studying by early evening and sleep.

## The morning of the exam

- Eat something that will last three hours.
- Arrive early. Check-in takes time, and personal items go in a locker.
- Bring your identification and nothing you are not allowed to take in.

## In the first minutes

- Use any tutorial screen to settle and check how to confirm answers.
- Remind yourself: the exam will feel hard because it adapts to you. ISC2 says everyone gets about half of the items they see right.
- Note the time remaining at the start so the checkpoints make sense.

## During the exam

- Read the last line of each stem first, then the whole stem, then decide before looking at the options.
- Look for the decisive words: FIRST, BEST, MOST, the role, the stage.
- Use the manager's order of thinking when two options look right: people, business, risk, management, policy, root cause.
- Do not spend more than about 90 seconds on any one item. You cannot come back.
- Do not try to guess your result from how many items you get or how hard the last one felt. Neither tells you anything reliable.
- If you take a break, the clock keeps running.

## Afterwards

- At a Pearson VUE centre, the proctor gives you an unofficial result as you check out. ISC2 emails the official result.
- **If you passed:** complete the certification application, including endorsement, within **nine months** of your exam date. You will need an endorser who is an ISC2 certified professional in good standing, or you can ask ISC2 to endorse you with proof of employment. If you do not yet have the experience, you become an Associate of ISC2.
- **If you did not pass:** you can retest after 30 test-free days following a first attempt, 60 after a second and 90 after a third or later, up to four attempts in 12 months. Use the time to work on the domains that felt weakest, and practise the no-review format.

## Last practice before the real one

A final timed run a few days before, not the night before, helps you trust your pacing. The [free CISSP practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cissp?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=557dqd&utm_content=cissp) is one option.
